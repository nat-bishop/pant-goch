AMinimum viable cable

Just the briefing. No "you are RAVEN" rules-frame, no roster, no meter glossary. Title is the op codename — no "ORIENTATION" eyebrow. Player learns the verb from cycle 1's draft, learns the cast by meeting them in dispatches, learns the HUD by hovering.

Op. LYNX-EXFIL
Bucharest, six cycles.
From: MERIWETHER, Director, Romanian Desk To: RAVEN, handler-of-record Re: OP. LYNX-EXFIL You have six cycles. Forty-eight hours ago the resident's people photographed LYNX at a meet that should not have happened. The image is in the Bucharest file. Counter-intel — Colonel MUNTEANU, name him plainly in your cables — has not moved yet. He will. The sweep is tightening; once LYNX is named in MUNTEANU's report the file closes. The eastern train is clean. The Vienna network is offline; do not ask. KESTREL is in the city on a separate reconnaissance, cover intact. She is yours to spend. Spend her carefully. Bring her home, RAVEN. Or do not. The choice is filed against your name. — M.
