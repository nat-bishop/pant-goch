Suspicion scores

Mechanic in the open

Each suspect has a hidden guilt flag. Investigations apply different deltas based on whether the suspect is innocent or guilty. The player sees the score change every cycle and reasons about where signal is coming from.

Threshold: 8. First suspect to cross is named.

— What's happening under the hood —

You are seeing the entire mechanic. Each click is a numeric bet. The prose is decoration over a Slay-the-Spire-shaped game. The player's reasoning is: "which action gives me the best signal for the lowest cost, given current bar positions?"

This is the version that could be replaced by a polished branching dialogue tree without much loss. Use this as a baseline to feel how the LLM disappears.

Option iii — Confidence builds. At 80% you deduce.
Both systems run. Investigations surface facts and advance a visible Operation Confidence bar. At 80%, the option set unlocks a finale: pick which officer to move on, using the facts you've gathered. Match the truth → success. Miss → failure.

Two systems running in parallel: facts (narrative) and confidence (mechanical). Confidence is the player's progress signal; facts are the deduction material. At 80%, a special finale option set appears: "Operations is ready. Who is the mole?" with A/B/C buttons. The choice is informed by the facts but is still ultimately a deduction.

Notice: the final deduction is a guess if the facts haven't really resolved it. This is the residual "submit a verdict" problem from earlier.
