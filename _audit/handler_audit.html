Three highest-leverage engine additions (genre-agnostic)

1 · First-class Clock type
A Clock primitive: name, segments, tick, fires_event_when_full. Replaces ad-hoc threshold patterns. From Blades in the Dark + Citizen Sleeper. Single cleanest type addition.
2 · Per-agent traits + injuries
Same agent + same option = different delta + different prose constraints depending on traits. Multiplies authored content by trait-cardinality without writing more options. From CK3, Battle Brothers, Wildermyth.
3 · meta_state across missions
The fired ledger and quality snapshots seed the next scenario's initial state. Engine already has the persistence layer; just don't call make_initial_state between campaigns. From Hades + Inscryption.
